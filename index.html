Who could help with your question?

Different people are useful for different kinds of questions. Choose the one that seems most helpful right now.

Academic advisor Helpful if you're exploring a major, minor, courses, requirements, or how an academic option could connect to your goals.
Professor or instructor Helpful if you want to understand a field of study, subject area, research area, graduate path, or what people do with expertise in that field.
Professional or alum Helpful if you want the inside view of a specific job or industry — daily work, work environment, skills, challenges, career paths, or how someone got started.
Career coach Helpful if you are still sorting through options, comparing possibilities, figuring out what information you need next, or finding someone to connect with.
Someone you already know Helpful when a friend, family member, coworker, supervisor, or other connection has experience that could give you a useful perspective.

What kind of experience would help?

You do not need a big internship to test an idea. A small experience can give you useful information.

Job shadow or observe Best when you want to see the day-to-day work, environment, pace, and interactions for yourself.
Volunteer Useful for testing your interest in a population, cause, setting, or type of work without a long commitment.
Attend an event or visit Useful for getting exposure to an industry, organization, employer, or professional community.
Try a small project Useful when you want to test whether you enjoy a skill or task that is common in the career.
Explore a student organization Useful for learning more about a field, meeting others with similar interests, and seeing what related experiences are available.
